## Task Management

### Task Lifecycle
1. **Backlog** — Task identified but not yet prioritized
2. **Planned** — Task prioritized for upcoming work
3. **In Progress** — Active development
4. **Blocked** — Cannot proceed due to external dependency
5. **Review** — Complete and awaiting review (if applicable)
6. **Done** — Completed and verified

### Priority Levels
- **High:** Critical for MVP or core functionality
- **Medium:** Important but not blocking
- **Low:** Nice to have, future enhancement
